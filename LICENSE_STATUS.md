explicit status statement, not a license grant. Delivery to the competition organizer is governed by
the applicable competition/submission terms and the owner's authorization.

the final solution's `LICENSES/` directory. Container base images and installed Python packages also

Before publishing or redistributing MemScope source outside the authorized evaluation channel, the
owner must select an appropriate project license or provide another explicit legal basis.
